Privacy Policy

Application: Wrong Way
Effective Date: November 27, 2026
Platforms: Android (Google Play & Huawei AppGallery) and Desktop

This Privacy Policy explains how Wrong Way ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and protects information when you play our game. You can play the full 60-level Campaign and Zone Creator completely offline as a Guest without creating an account.

1. Information We Collect

  • Local Device Storage (Offline Gameplay): Game progress (unlocked levels, earned stars, best completion times, death counts, tutorial status, language, audio preferences, custom touch-control layouts, and Zone Creator custom levels) is stored locally on your device.
  • Google Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google to enable cloud save sync and Global/Friends Leaderboards, we receive your basic Google profile information: your email address and display name. We do not access your Google contacts, photos, calendar, or Drive files.
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Profile Data: When online sync is active, we store a player tag (e.g., WW-1234), chosen username, selected avatar index, and campaign progress metrics (stars, completion times, death counts) so you can restore your progress across devices and appear on leaderboards.
  • Advertising Data (Yandex Mobile Ads): Our mobile version displays optional rewarded video ads (such as ad-assisted level unlocks) and periodic interstitial ads through Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. The ad SDK may automatically collect device identifiers (such as OAID/AAID), IP address, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure ads.

2. Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following trusted third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In / Android Credential Manager: Used solely for optional player authentication.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase: Used as our cloud database backend to store player profiles, cloud progress backups, and leaderboard rankings.
    Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads: Used to deliver interstitial and rewarded advertisements on mobile devices.
    Privacy Policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

3. How We Use Information

  • Save and synchronize your game progress across devices when signed in with Google.
  • Display Global and Friends leaderboards ranked by stars, completion time, and fewest deaths.
  • Support free gameplay through rewarded and interstitial advertising on mobile devices.
  • Maintain service reliability and prevent cheating or corrupted save states.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Local save data remains on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the Game. You can sign out of your Google account at any time from the in-game Profile screen to return to Guest mode.

To request permanent deletion of your cloud profile and leaderboard data stored on Supabase, please contact us at the email address below with your Google email or Player Tag (WW-XXXX). Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Wrong Way is a general-audience arcade platformer and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction) without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information via Google Sign-In, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

6. Data Security

All network communications with Supabase and authentication endpoints use HTTPS/TLS encryption. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new game features or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
